# Code of Conduct

This project should be a place where people can collaborate, disagree, and contribute without making the experience unnecessarily unpleasant for others.

Please:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Criticize ideas, code, and decisions rather than the people behind them.
- Assume good faith where reasonable.
- Keep disagreements focused on the issue at hand.
- Be mindful that tone can be difficult to read online.
- Accept that maintainers and contributors may make decisions you disagree with.

Direct criticism and technical disagreement are welcome. Personal attacks, insults, harassment, discrimination, ridicule, trolling, deliberate provocation, or unnecessarily hostile and dismissive behaviour are not.

You do not have to agree with everyone, and you do not have to like every decision. You are expected to communicate in a way that allows the project to remain productive and welcoming to contributors.

These guidelines are not exhaustive. Maintainers may use their judgment and take reasonable action at their discretion when behaviour is disruptive, hostile, or otherwise harmful to the project or its community. This may include removing content or restricting participation where appropriate.
